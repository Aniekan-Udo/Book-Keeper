**Book-Keeper** is run by **[legal name of your business]**, [address], Nigeria.
Questions or requests: **[email]** or WhatsApp **[number]**.

## What Book-Keeper does
It keeps your business records from the messages, voice notes and screenshots you send
it, reminds your customers to pay when you ask it to, and makes statements you can share.
**It never holds, sends or collects money for you.**

## What we collect
- **About you:** your phone number, name, business name, and email if you use the website.
- **What you send us:** your messages, voice notes, and the bank or POS alert screenshots
  and texts you forward — and the business records made from them (sales, expenses,
  debts, loans).
- **About your customers and suppliers:** the names and phone numbers *you* give us, and
  what they owe or are owed. Please only give us numbers you're allowed to use for
  reminding people about money they owe you.
- **From bank alerts:** only the amount, date, name and description. We don't keep account
  balances or account numbers.

We never ask for your PIN, password, OTP, BVN or card number. Don't send them to us.

## Why
To keep your records, answer your questions about your business, send the reminders you
approve, and improve how well Book-Keeper understands messages. **[During the pilot, our
team reads messages to fix mistakes.]** We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

- **Meta (WhatsApp)** carries your messages to us.
- **Google** processes messages, voice notes and screenshots with AI to understand them.

## Your customers
When you ask us to remind a customer, we send them a WhatsApp message in your business's
name. They can reply **STOP** at any time and will never get another reminder from us.

## How long we keep it
While your account is open. [If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your records
within [30] days, except anything we must keep by law.] Backups are deleted after [30] days.

## Your rights
You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it — message
us at [contact]. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the **Nigeria Data
Protection Commission (NDPC)**.
