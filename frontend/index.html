Under the hood

A small team of agents, one shared memory

Each agent does one job. They never pass notes to each other: what one learns, the others read from a shared memory graph on the next message.

Books

Recorder

Turns your messages into ledger entries, corrections and reports. Asks when a name could be two people.

Debts

Collector

Settles promises as their dates pass, ranks who to chase with reasons, drafts reminders you approve.

Bank alerts

Alert matcher

Reads forwarded alerts, flags fake "transfer successful" screens, learns which bank name is which customer.

Memory

Memory curator

After every reply, decides what's worth remembering. Proposals are checked by code before anything is saved.

Graph memory on FalkorDB. People, promises, nicknames, habits and reminders are connected, timestamped and traced to the message they came from. Your money stays in the ledger; the graph holds what the agents know.

Trust

Built to be safe with your business and your customers

It never touches money

Book-Keeper only keeps records, reports and reminders. No payments, no transfers, no payment links, and it will never ask for your PIN, OTP, BVN or bank login.

You approve every reminder

Reminders are drafted, shown to you, and only sent after your yes. Customers can reply STOP, and there are daily limits and cooldowns.

Every memory is traceable

Each thing it remembers links to the message you sent and the agent that saved it. Wrong? Say so and it's corrected.

Your books stay yours

Each business has its own separate memory. Customer statement links are private, expire, and can be withdrawn.

Questions

Good to know

Does Book-Keeper handle my money?

No. It only keeps records, produces reports and drafts reminders. Payments always happen directly between you and your customers.

Do I need WhatsApp?

No. The web app does everything. WhatsApp is there for traders who'd rather not open a browser, and both share the same books and memory.

What if it gets something wrong?

Say "cancel that" or tell it the right amount or name. When a name could be two people or an amount is missing, it asks instead of guessing. It never records an amount you didn't give.

How does the memory work?

Each business has its own memory graph on FalkorDB. The agents read it before replying and a curator adds to it after, with every memory linked to the message it came from. The People tab in the app shows it all.

What languages does it understand?

English, Nigerian Pidgin and a mix of both, with shorthand like "5k", "2m", "balance" and "on credit". Voice notes work too.
